01

Purchasing & Receiving

Visibility and control from order creation to delivery confirmation.

Purchasing

  • ✓All vendors in one place — no punchouts
  • ✓Existing contract prices, delivery days, and cut-off times honored
  • ✓GPO compliance tracking
  • ✓Supplier performance and pricing trend visibility
  • ✓HSM Catalog Integration
  • ✓Purchase from all suppliers in a single checkout experience
  • ✓Mobile-friendly purchasing from any device
  • ✓Order guides that include multiple suppliers
  • ✓Search across similar products for cost optimization
  • ✓AI ordering that remembers user preferences to make routine orders simple

Controls & approvals

  • ✓Configurable multi-level approval workflows (property → corporate)
  • ✓Route approvals based on % of budget, dollar amount, supplier, and user
  • ✓Real-time budget visibility linked to GL structure

Receiving

  • ✓Mobile receiving workflow
  • ✓Capture missing items, damages, substitutions, and credits in real time
  • ✓Automatic inventory updates on successful receipt
  • ✓Discrepancy tracking and supplier dispute management
02

AP Automation

Make invoice processing fast, accurate, and audit-ready.

Capture

  • ✓Unique email address per property for vendors to send invoices directly into Reeco
  • ✓Upload paper invoices by taking pictures with Reeco's mobile app
  • ✓Automatic extraction of all invoice data including line items
  • ✓Duplicate invoice detection and flagging
  • ✓Capture vendor statements and identify any missing invoices

Coding & allocation

  • ✓AI-powered GL code assignment — learns and improves over time
  • ✓Split allocations across multiple cost centers
  • ✓Custom coding rules by vendor, category, or property

Accounting sync

  • ✓Real-time API sync with M3
  • ✓Budget-vs-actual visibility pulled directly from accounting

Approve & audit

  • ✓Approval workflows routed by amount, vendor, or department
  • ✓Three-way matching: PO vs. receipt vs. invoice
03

Inventory Management

Real-time inventory visibility with a mobile-first experience.

Counting

  • ✓Count from any mobile device
  • ✓Keep counting even without a connection — counts sync automatically once you're back online
  • ✓Multiple inventory counts can run simultaneously across your team
  • ✓Assign an audit owner and counters per storeroom — assigned counters get notified and see only their areas of focus
  • ✓Set audit frequency per storeroom — daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or quarterly
  • ✓Storeroom-based audits
  • ✓Beverage and alcohol tracking

Tracking

  • ✓Transfer items between storerooms — on-hand value updates in both locations automatically
  • ✓Waste and spoilage tracking
  • ✓Always know what was delivered since the last count

Valuation & insight

  • ✓Automatic value calculations using real purchase prices
  • ✓Variance analysis based on inventory counts vs. theoretical quantities from sales data
  • ✓Storeroom value trend — shows value change % vs. the prior audit, color-coded to flag deviations
  • ✓Order to PAR — generate a replenishment order directly from a completed audit based on PAR levels
04

Culinary

Connect menus to recipes with clear visibility into food costs.

Recipes

  • ✓Create and manage standardized recipes and sub-recipes
  • ✓Upload an existing recipe file — AI converts it into a structured recipe with ingredients, quantities, and instructions automatically
  • ✓Step-by-step cooking instructions for consistent kitchen execution
  • ✓Flexible units of measurement — AI understands real kitchen conversions and recalculates cost live when you change the UoM
  • ✓Print-friendly recipe cards for kitchen teams
  • ✓Allergen and nutrition tagging

Costing & margins

  • ✓Recipe costs update in real time as ingredient prices change
  • ✓Ingredient price history — see price trends over time directly within the recipe
  • ✓Multi-component dishes — link multiple sub-recipes to a single menu item
  • ✓Dish-level cost-vs.-net-sales comparison
  • ✓Cost alerts when ingredient prices change
  • ✓POS sync — menu items mapped directly to recipe costs

Menus

  • ✓Menus page synced to your POS — every menu item, its linked recipe, units sold, and food cost % in one view
  • ✓Date-range filter on menu performance — analyze food cost over any time window
  • ✓Food cost % color coding — instant visual signal for items above your target margin
